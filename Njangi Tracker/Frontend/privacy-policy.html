Privacy Policy

Njangi Tracker stores account, member, contribution, cycle and loan information to operate the group management system. Access is limited by account role.

Information we store

Names, contact details, authentication data and records entered by administrators or members.

Password security

Passwords and password-reset PINs are stored as cryptographic hashes. Password-reset PINs expire after 10 minutes.

Your responsibility

Only enter information that your group is authorized to manage. Keep account credentials private.
