FEATURES

Tools for the work your group already does

Keep the core Njangi records in one system instead of spreading them across notebooks and separate spreadsheets.

MB

Member management

Add, edit, search and remove members while keeping their group information together.

CT

Contribution tracking

Record payments, review payment status and keep contribution history available to administrators.

CY

Cycle management

Create cycles, set the target pool, assign the planned recipient and close completed cycles.

RH

Payment reliability

Calculate reliability from completed cycles and use the result when reviewing loan requests.

LN

Emergency loans

Record loan requests, review eligibility, approve or reject requests and record repayments.

HS

Payment history

Search contribution records by member and date so administrators can review what was recorded.
